What is sound design?

Four layers.
One finished sound.

AI videos usually come out silent, or with sound that doesn't match the picture. Sound design is everything you hear, built for every second of your video.

01

Ambience

The world around the picture: rain, a city, a forest, the hum of a spaceship.

02

Sound effects

Every action gets its sound: footsteps, doors, whooshes, impacts.

03

Music

The emotion and the rhythm: a build, tension, the drop on the right frame.

04

Mix

Everything balanced and at the right loudness for TikTok, YouTube or an ad.
